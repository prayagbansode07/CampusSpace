How can we help you?

Find answers to common questions about finding rooms, making reservations, and managing your bookings. If a problem continues, contact your campus administrator.

Find a Room

  • Open the Rooms page.
  • Select your preferred date and time.
  • Review the room details and availability.

Book a Room

  • Select an available room.
  • Complete the booking form accurately.
  • Submit the form and check My Bookings.

Manage Bookings

  • Open My Bookings to review reservations.
  • Check the booking status before visiting the room.
  • Use the available cancellation option if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I book a room?

The room may already be reserved, conflict with a scheduled lecture, or be unavailable for your selected time. Try another time or room.

Why was my booking blocked by a faculty reservation?

CampusSpace gives approved faculty bookings priority over overlapping student bookings. Students cannot override a confirmed faculty reservation.

Why is my faculty account not allowed to use priority?

Faculty priority is available only to approved faculty accounts. Contact your campus administrator if your approval status appears incorrect.

Where can I check my booking status?

Open My Bookings to review your reservations and their current statuses.

What should I do if something goes wrong?

Note the room, selected date and time, and any error message. Share these details with your campus administrator so they can investigate the problem.

Still need help?

For account approval issues, booking conflicts, or technical problems, contact your campus administrator. Include the relevant room, date, time, and error message where possible.
